# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing funnel performance and conversion trends using Power BI. The dashboard tracks customer journeys from impressions to conversions, helping identify drop-off points and optimize marketing strategies.

## Objectives

* Analyze funnel performance
* Measure conversion rates
* Evaluate campaign effectiveness
* Track ROI and revenue generation
* Identify high-performing marketing channels

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Excel
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Marketing Overview

* Total Impressions
* Total Clicks
* Total Leads
* Total Customers
* Revenue Analysis
* ROI Tracking

### 2. Funnel Analysis

* Marketing Funnel Visualization
* Conversion Rate Analysis
* Channel Performance
* Campaign Comparison

### 3. Campaign Performance & Insights

* ROI by Campaign
* Revenue by Channel
* Campaign Effectiveness
* Business Recommendations

## Key Insights

* High-performing channels generated stronger conversions.
* Funnel analysis identified customer drop-off stages.
* Certain campaigns delivered higher ROI.
* Conversion rates varied across channels and regions.
